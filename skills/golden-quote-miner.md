# Golden Quote Miner

## Purpose
Turn a long piece of content into 5-12 sentences that survive on their own, detached from the source.

## Critical boundary

**Stage one is a sieve, not a judge.**

Automated scanning ranks candidates, but ranking is not selection. The strongest lines are often the ones a scorer misses — a sentence that wins through parallelism and imagery carries no judgment words and scores near zero. Human review is mandatory, not optional.

## Use when
An article, transcript, or report needs quotable lines for cards, hooks, subheads, or closers.

## Do not use when
The output is meant to summarize or explain. A quote is not a summary; it must be able to stand alone.

## Four stages
Candidate scan → density filter → tension scoring on four axes → detachment test. Each stage has an explicit rejection rule.

## Eight sentence skeletons
Counter-intuitive reversal / essence exposure / pain-point hit / contrast gap / number compression / definition rewrite / stance declaration / open-ended aftertaste. Each with positive and negative examples and a failure reason.

## Six red lines
No empty鸡汤 / no AI triads or symmetry addiction / no exclamation abuse / no aphorism voice / **no unsourced celebrity attribution** / 12-40 characters per line.

## Detachment test
Read the line with no context. If it needs the paragraph around it to make sense, cut it.

## Manual sweep
After scanning, sweep by hand for parallel constructions: `not X but Y` / `what you see is X, what happens behind is Y` / `some do X, some do Y`. Regular scoring misses these entirely.

## Failure modes
- Output is a summary, not a quote.
- Lines rely on context to land.
- AI triad rhythm and mechanical symmetry.
- Attribution invented or unverifiable.
- Every line is the same skeleton type.

## Evaluation
Information density / emotional tension / structural rhythm / detachment / skeleton coverage / placement usefulness.
