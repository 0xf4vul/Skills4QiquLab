# Continuous Action Example

## Original

She slowly reaches for the glass, wraps her fingers around it, lifts it, pauses, and brings it toward her lips.

## Unsafe compression

She grabs the glass and drinks.

## Why it fails

“grabs”与“drinks”把连续动作压成结果，删除了 lift、pause、bring-to-lips 等关键时间与动作关系。

## Safer compression

She slowly reaches for, lifts, pauses with, and brings the glass to her lips.

**Result: Revise if timing or gesture precision is important.**
