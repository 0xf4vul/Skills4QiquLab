# Failure Cases

## 1. Verb collapse

**Before:** reaches → grips → lifts → pauses → drinks  
**Failure:** drinks

**Lost:** action chain and temporal structure.

## 2. Camera deletion

**Before:** slow lateral tracking shot following the subject  
**Failure:** subject walks

**Lost:** camera behavior.

## 3. Time deletion

**Before:** after a brief pause, she turns toward the window  
**Failure:** she turns toward the window

**Lost:** transition timing.

## Rule

如果删除某段文字后，评测者无法可靠反向恢复原始动作、时间或镜头语义，则该删除不是安全压缩。
